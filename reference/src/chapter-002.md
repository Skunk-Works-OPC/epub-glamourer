# Colour in Publishing

Colour is a fundamental aspect of visual communication in publishing. The palette chosen for a book's interior design shapes the reader's emotional response before a single word is read.

The glamour stylesheet employs a carefully considered palette of five primary colours:

**Cream** (#faf7f2) serves as the page background — warm, not stark white, echoing the feel of quality laid paper. It reduces eye fatigue during extended reading sessions.

**Ink** (#1c1c1e) is used for body text — softer than pure black, it creates a more refined reading experience while maintaining excellent contrast against the cream ground.

**Navy** (#1e3a5f) anchors headings and navigational elements. Deep and authoritative, it projects the confidence of traditional publishing without the coldness of pure black.

**Gold** (#b8924a) provides the accent — used sparingly for drop capitals, ornamental dividers, and decorative borders on the title page. Gold signals quality and draws the reader's eye to structural moments in the text.

**Sage** (#4a6741) offers an alternative accent for secondary elements, providing a natural, grounded counterpoint to the warmth of gold.

## Responsive Design for Reading Systems

A well-designed EPUB adapts gracefully to every device: a compact e-reader screen, a tablet held in portrait or landscape orientation, or a desktop caliber viewer. The glamour stylesheet implements three responsive breakpoints:

- **Desktop** (>900px): Full margins, 2em padding, maximum reading width of 1100px
- **Tablet** (551–900px): Reduced padding, stacked illustration tables
- **Mobile** (≤550px): Compact padding, left-aligned text, full-width images
